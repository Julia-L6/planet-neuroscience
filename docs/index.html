    Oxytocin Signaling Bidirectionally Modulates Serotonergic and Dopaminergic Neurons in the Dorsal Raphe Nucleus

    The involvement of oxytocin in social behavior is well established, yet its interaction with neuromodulatory systems, such as serotonin and dopamine, remains incompletely understood. Here, we utilized immunohistochemistry, anatomical tracing, chemogenetic, and ex vivo electrophysiological approaches primarily in male mice, with female mice used only for an additional anatomical tracing control experiment, investigating how oxytocin modulates serotonergic and dopaminergic neuronal populations. Employing the dual-eGRASP tool, we visualized oxytocinergic projections from the paraventricular nucleus (PVN) to the serotonergic and dopaminergic neurons in dorsal raphe nucleus (DRN). Blocking oxytocin receptors in the DRN attenuated social interaction behavior. Additionally, analysis of c-Fos levels and neuronal excitability revealed that oxytocin inhibits serotonergic neurons while activating dopaminergic neurons in the DRN. Our findings suggest a bidirectional regulatory role of oxytocin signaling in the DRN serotonergic and dopaminergic neurons.

    Evaluation of the Antibiofilm and Antimicrobial Effects of Selenium Nanoparticles on Multidrug-Resistant Salmonella Isolates [version 2; peer review: 2 approved with reservations, 1 not approved]

    Background and Aim Salmonella is a major cause of bloodstream infections and foodborne diseases worldwide. The rise of multidrug-resistant (MDR) strains and biofilm formation complicates treatment and public health management. This study aimed to evaluate the effects of biosynthesized selenium nanoparticles (SeNPs) on the biofilm-associated gene expression of MDR Salmonella isolates. Methods Thirty-two Salmonella isolates were recovered from blood samples in Baghdad, Iraq. Biofilm formation was assessed using Congo Red and microtiter plate assays. Antimicrobial susceptibility testing was performed for multiple antibiotics. SeNPs were biosynthesized and characterized using scanning electron microscopy (SEM) and applied at fractional MIC concentrations (12.5%, 25%, 50%, and 100%) for three representative MDR, strong biofilm-forming isolates. RT-qPCR was conducted to quantify the expression of biofilm-associated genes csgD and ssrB, using gapA as a housekeeping gene. Results All isolates formed biofilms, with 75% classified as strong formers. MIC values for SeNPs were determined for the selected isolates. RT-qPCR analysis revealed a dose-dependent downregulation of csgD expression, reaching statistical significance at all tested SeNP concentrations, whereas ssrB expression exhibited a non-significant decreasing trend. Higher SeNP concentrations resulted in stronger suppression of csgD. Conclusions Biosynthesized SeNPs exhibited in vitro modulatory effects on biofilm-associated gene expression in MDR isolates. These findings provide preliminary evidence of a potential antibiofilm regulatory effect, while direct antibacterial activity and in vivo efficacy require further investigation.

    Determinants of Digital Marketing Adoption and MSME Performance in Indonesia’s Halal Food Sector  [version 2; peer review: 1 approved with reservations]

    Background Digital transformation is a key driver of Indonesia’s economy, contributing an estimated USD 130 billion by 2025. Halal MSMEs in the food and beverage sector contribute approximately USD 180 billion annually, account for 60% of GDP, and employ nearly 97% of the workforce. However, digital marketing adoption remains constrained by digital literacy, infrastructure, financial, and sharia-related challenges. This study examines the determinants of digital marketing adoption and its impact on halal MSME performance. Methods This study employed an explanatory quantitative research design based on the Technology–Organization–Environment–Personal (TOEP) framework. Data were collected from 500 halal-certified MSMEs operating in the food and beverage sector across Indonesia using a structured questionnaire. Structural Equation Modeling–Partial Least Squares (SEM-PLS) with SmartPLS 4.0 was used to evaluate the relationships among technological, organizational, environmental, and personal factors, digital marketing adoption, and business performance. Results The findings indicate that technological, organizational, environmental, and personal factors significantly influence digital marketing adoption among halal MSMEs. All four dimensions showed positive and statistically significant relationships with adoption; however, complexity had a positive coefficient (β = 0.128, p = 0.010), contrary to the negative relationship hypothesized in H3. Furthermore, digital marketing adoption has a positive and significant effect on business performance by improving operational efficiency, increasing customer loyalty, strengthening halal brand visibility, and enhancing overall business competitiveness. Conclusions Digital marketing adoption is a key strategic driver for improving the competitiveness and sustainability of halal MSMEs in Indonesia. Strengthening digital capabilities through technological readiness, organizational support, favorable business environments, and personal competencies is essential to accelerate digital transformation and maximize the contribution of halal MSMEs to the national digital economy. The findings also highlight the importance of examining personal factors alongside technological, organizational, and environmental conditions when understanding digital marketing adoption among halal MSMEs.

    Background Organizational agility is important for hospitals facing service complexity, regulatory demands, and environmental change. Methods A cross-sectional organizational survey was analyzed using partial least squares structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM) in SmartPLS 4. The original interim extract included 81 records, of which one (R001, 5 January 2026) was a pre-survey test submission. The corrected primary analysis includes 80 eligible key-informant responses and retains the final 29-indicator reflective global measurement model. The global innovation ambidexterity construct represents joint exploratory and exploitative indicator levels and does not directly test a balance between the two orientations.

    Optimization of Exact Feedback Linearization EKF Controller for Magnetic Levitation Using Cuckoo Search Algorithm [version 2; peer review: 1 approved with reservations]

    Background Magnetic levitation is an electromechanical technique that allows objects to float in air using magnetic fields alone. This technology eliminates physical contact, thereby reducing friction, wear, and energy loss while providing high-precision and stable operation. A key challenge in maglev systems lies in their inherent instability and nonlinearity, which complicate control design. In this study, a hollow metal sphere is levitated as part of a real-time experimental setup, which requires a fine balance between gravitational and magnetic forces. Method To address the complexities of the Magnetic levitation system, an intelligent control strategy is employed. The core control architecture integrates an Extended Kalman Filter for accurate state estimation with Exact Feedback Linearization to manage the nonlinear dynamics of the system. Additionally, system performance is optimized through Cuckoo Search, a nature-inspired metaheuristic algorithm used for pole placement tuning within the feedback linearization framework. The Extended Kalman Filter estimates hidden states and compensates for noise and disturbances, while the Exact Feedback Linearization simplifies the system dynamics to a linear form for more straightforward controller design. The Cuckoo Search algorithm ensures optimal controller parameters by minimizing a performance index. Result The combined Cuckoo Search-optimized, Exact Feedback Linearization with Extended Kalman Filter demonstrates significant improvements in the robustness and stability of the Magnetic levitation system. Simulation and experimental results show enhanced tracking performance, faster settling time, and improved disturbance rejection compared to conventional methods. The system remains stable and responsive under various operating conditions, validating the effectiveness of the proposed approach. Conclusion The study presents a robust and intelligent control framework for magnetic levitation systems. The method effectively addresses the challenges posed by system nonlinearity and uncertainty, leading to improved control accuracy and system performance. These findings suggest that such intelligent control strategies are well-suited for practical applications of Magnetic levitation systems, will enhance reliability.

    Background Public financial management has increasingly moved beyond a narrow compliance-oriented perspective toward broader governance arrangements involving transparency, oversight, participation, digitalization, and multiple accountability actors. This study maps the development of research on public financial management and formulates a conceptual agenda for collaborative financial governance. Methods A bibliometric-systematic literature review was conducted using Scopus-indexed journal articles published in English between 2022 and 2026. After database filtering and eligibility screening, 265 articles were retained for bibliometric performance analysis and science mapping using Biblioshiny and VOSviewer. A second-stage screening identified 39 studies with direct thematic relevance for the systematic review and in-depth full-text thematic synthesis. Results Publication output increased markedly after 2023 and peaked in 2025. Fiscal transparency, local government, accountability, budget transparency, audit, oversight, participation, public spending, and digital openness emerged as dominant and interconnected themes. The systematic synthesis indicates a shift from primarily compliance-based approaches toward broader multi-actor accountability and identifies six recurring governance domains: compliance, audit, transparency, participation, digitalization, and accountability. Conclusions Collaborative financial governance is conceptualized as an integrated governance architecture in which these six domains operate as complementary mechanisms. The framework extends conventional public financial management by linking institutional control, fiscal openness, stakeholder involvement, digital support, and accountability to strengthen the legitimacy, responsiveness, and effectiveness of public resource management.

    Are instructional leadership, BOS fund utilization, and teacher reflection associated with literacy and numeracy achievement? [version 2; peer review: 2 approved with reservations]

    Background Improving literacy and numeracy remains a central priority in Indonesian education reform, yet evidence on how school-level managerial and instructional factors relate to these outcomes remains limited, particularly within the National Assessment framework. Methods This quantitative explanatory study analyzed secondary administrative panel data comprising 2,952 school-year observations from elementary, junior high, and senior high schools in Bangka Belitung Islands Province, Indonesia, during 2022–2024. Data were obtained from Rapor Pendidikan, Dapodik, and BOS financial reporting systems. Panel regression and regression-based mediation analyses were conducted using EViews to examine the associations among principal leadership, BOS fund utilization, teacher reflection, learning quality, and student literacy and numeracy achievement. School-community support was included as a control variable in the direct-association models. Results Principal leadership, teacher reflection, and learning quality were positively associated with both literacy and numeracy achievement. Teacher reflection and learning quality showed particularly strong positive associations with the two learning outcomes. By contrast, the proportion of BOS expenditure allocated to quality-improvement activities showed small but statistically significant negative associations with literacy, numeracy, and teacher reflection. The mediation analysis identified statistically significant negative indirect associations between BOS fund utilization and both literacy and numeracy achievement through teacher reflection. The direct associations remained statistically significant after teacher reflection was included, indicating complementary partial mediation in statistical terms. Conclusions The findings indicate that a greater proportion of BOS expenditure allocated to quality-improvement activities is not necessarily accompanied by higher literacy and numeracy achievement within the observed period. These associations should not be interpreted as evidence that BOS expenditure reduces learning outcomes. Rather, they highlight the importance of considering how financial resources are translated into instructional practices, teacher professional development, and learning quality. Strengthening instructional leadership, reflective teaching cultures, learning quality, and the pedagogical use of BOS funds may support improvements in literacy and numeracy in Indonesian schools.

    Abstract* Background Indigenous knowledge plays a crucial role in primary education by strengthening cultural identity, supporting sustainability, and enabling contextualized learning.     Pregnancy with Osteogenesis Imperfecta: A Scoping Review [version 2; peer review: 1 approved, 1 approved with reservations]

    Purpose Osteogenesis imperfecta (OI) is a rare genetic disorder characterized by genotype and phenotypic heterogeneity. Limited literature is available regarding pregnancy with OI. Objectives To map observational evidence concerning pregnancy, childbirth, maternal fractures, and neonatal outcomes among women with OI. Methods A scoping review methodology was used to record and summarize the existing research evidence. The review process was conducted following the PRISMA-ScR guidelines. Three databases (Pubmed, Embase, web of science) were searched systematically. Literature published between 1975-2025 was extracted. Original studies mentioning OI with a minimum of 25 pregnancies were included. Results 546 articles were identified for title and abstracts screening. 28 records were reviewed for full text; seven studies met the inclusion criteria and were included. Reference details, study characteristics, topics of interest, and main findings are presented. Cesarean delivery was frequent, but estimates differed by setting and denominator. In the Danish population-based study, 31% of births in the parental-OI cohort were by cesarean compared with 19% in the reference population; a US inpatient cohort reported 75% among maternal-OI deliveries. The US inpatient study found increased odds of antepartum hemorrhage (OR 2.01), placental abruption (OR 2.50), fetal growth restriction/small-for-gestational-age birth (OR 2.42), and preterm birth (OR 2.24), whereas Danish registry findings were generally more reassuring. Fracture results differed: a French bone-center cohort reported pregnancy/postpartum fractures in 12/50 women (24%), whereas a Danish self-controlled analysis found no significant increase in fracture incidence relative to preconception (12-month IRR 1.00; 95% CI 0.42–2.40). Reporting by OI phenotype was incomplete. Conclusion The literature indicates high cesarean use and identifies potentially important maternal and neonatal complications, but their frequency and relationship to OI severity remain uncertain. Standardized prospective, phenotype-stratified studies are needed; current data do not justify uniform disease-specific delivery or breastfeeding recommendations.

    Sales growth conditions the relationship between cost management and business liquidity [version 1; peer review: awaiting peer review]

    Background Cost management and business liquidity are determinants of the stability of industrial firms, but little is known about how it depends on the commercial context. It was determined whether cost management influences the liquidity of an industrial firm in Pomalca, Peru (2011–2025), under the moderating effect of inventory turnover and sales growth. Methods An applied, quantitative, non-experimental, longitudinal and correlational-explanatory study with the financial statements of the firm (2011–2025, census sampling, n = 15), analyzed by means of a documentary file and linear regression with robust HAC/Newey-West errors in R 4.6.1. Results Cost management did not show a direct effect on current liquidity (β = 0.072; p = 0.146), but its interaction with sales growth was significant (β = 0.001; p = 0.009). Current liquidity, acid test, and net working capital significantly influenced cost management (p = 0.043; 0.040; 0.003), moderated by sales growth (adjusted R2 64.0%–68.9%). Conclusions Cost management does not directly influence liquidity; its effect only appears with sales growth, while liquidity does directly influence cost management, which functions as a limiting condition of this relationship.

    Nipa Palm (Nypa fruticans) Utilization: Implications for Blue Carbon and the Sustainable Bioeconomy – A Systematic Review [version 1; peer review: awaiting peer review]

    Background Nypa fruticans (nipa palm) provides ecological and economic benefits in tropical mangrove ecosystems but remains underrepresented in blue carbon inventories and bioeconomy policy. This review assessed its uses, carbon sequestration potential relative to other mangroves, and contribution to a sustainable bioeconomy. Methods A systematic review followed PRISMA 2020. Scopus was searched for “Nypa fruticans,” “Nipa Palm,” and “Nipah.” English-language, open-access journal articles published from 2016 to 2026 were screened in six stages using predefined criteria. Data were extracted into a structured database and analysed using bibliometric analysis and qualitative synthesis. Results Of 454 records identified, 175 studies were included. Research mainly originated from Indonesia, Malaysia, and Thailand, with publication output increasing after 2019. Eight themes emerged: bioenergy; food and functional products; health and pharmaceutical products; materials; livelihoods and value chains; cultural ecosystem services; blue carbon and ecosystem support; and sustainability risks, including invasiveness. Nipa may contribute to carbon storage in degraded coastal areas and dense stands, while bioethanol from its sap may achieve a net-negative carbon balance. Estimated annual net income from nipa-sap bioethanol was IDR 6.1–42.0 million ha−1. Its phytochemicals indicate antidiabetic, antimicrobial, and neuroprotective potential. Conclusions Nypa fruticans is a multifunctional coastal resource, but economic uses must be balanced against ecological trade-offs, including invasiveness outside its native range. REDD+ and Nationally Determined Contributions carbon accounting should consider nipa-specific values; soil carbon in nipa-dominated stands requires further study. Product innovation and adaptive coastal management could support a sustainable bioeconomy.

    Bone Mineral Density Across the Menopausal Transition: A Multi-Ethnic DXA Study from the Northern Emirates, UAE [version 1; peer review: awaiting peer review]

    Background Population-specific data on bone mineral density (BMD) among women in the Northern Emirates of the United Arab Emirates (UAE) remain limited, constraining evidence-based screening strategies in a multi-ethnic setting. This study quantified the prevalence of osteopenia and osteoporosis across menopausal status and examined the relative contributions of body mass index (BMI), ethnicity, and medical history to low BMD. Methodology A cross-sectional analysis of 161 women aged 29–64 years was conducted, who underwent dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA) of the lumbar spine and femoral neck between January and December 2023. BMD was classified according to World Health Organization criteria. Associations with menopausal status, BMI, self-reported region of origin, and past medical history were evaluated using chi-square tests, one-way ANOVA, Pearson correlations, bootstrap resampling (1,000 iterations), and multivariable logistic regression. Results Postmenopausal women exhibited a markedly higher prevalence of low BMD (osteopenia or osteoporosis) than premenopausal women (84.6% vs. 50.9%; p < 0.001). Mean BMI was significantly higher among women with normal BMD compared with those at risk (34.98 vs. 30.02 kg·m−2), with non-overlapping bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. BMI correlated positively with lumbar-spine T-scores (r = 0.31) and femoral-neck T-scores (r = 0.16). Substantial regional disparities were observed: women from the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) and Asia demonstrated the highest burden of low BMD (83% and 79%, respectively), whereas African women had the greatest proportion of normal BMD (54%). Conclusion In this multi-ethnic cohort from the Northern Emirates, menopausal status, BMI, and region of origin emerged as the dominant determinants of BMD, while isolated comorbidities contributed little additional predictive value. These findings support simplified, targeted DXA referral strategies based on readily available demographic and anthropometric factors and highlight the importance of maintaining BMI within an upper-normal range to mitigate postmenopausal bone loss.     The Two Dam Paradox in Sumbawa: Rethinking Irrigation Performance through Pastoral Livelihoods and Institutional Resilience [version 1; peer review: awaiting peer review]

    Large dams are conventionally judged by storage, irrigation coverage, hydropower, and flood-control outputs, although these metrics can conceal redistribution within coupled crop-livestock systems. This critical mini-review integrates international scholarship with an author-provided comparative dataset covering 367 households around the Beringin Sila and Bintang Bano dams on Sumbawa Island, Indonesia. Literature and technical records were retained when their bibliographic identity, digital object identifier, repository record, or responsible-agency provenance could be verified. The case evidence reports a decline in mean herd size from 12.4 to 7.1 head per household near Beringin Sila, whereas the corresponding estimate near Bintang Bano increased from 9.3 to 17.2 head. The contrast coincided with higher purchased-feed expenditure and weaker livestock-oriented collective action near Beringin Sila, while stronger cooperatives and mutual aid accompanied herd expansion near Bintang Bano. These descriptive patterns do not establish dam causality because the baseline was retrospective, panel continuity was not demonstrated, and service delivery changed during the observation period. Nevertheless, the wider evidence supports a coherent mechanism: irrigation intensification may compress seasonal grazing windows and individualize access, while institutions that coordinate forage, labor, security, and markets can buffer transition costs. The apparent paradox is therefore reframed as an infrastructure-institution mismatch rather than a binary contrast between successful and failed dams. A four-domain appraisal framework is proposed, integrating technical delivery, continuity of livestock resource functions, distributional effects, and institutional capacity. Dam governance in dryland crop-livestock regions should protect seasonal mobility, finance feasible feed transitions, connect water-user and livestock organizations, and monitor herd outcomes alongside irrigated hectares.

    Cellular Uptake and Intracellular Distribution of Phycocyanobilin in PC12 Cells: Spatial Association with Redox- and Mitochondrial-Related Regions [version 1; peer review: awaiting peer review]

    Background Phycocyanobilin (PCB) is a linear tetrapyrrolic chromophore derived from C-phycocyanin with reported antioxidant and neuroprotective properties. However, its cellular uptake and intracellular distribution in neuronal cells remain poorly characterized. This study aimed to characterize PCB internalization in PC12 cells and to examine its spatial association with DHE- and JC-1-positive intracellular regions. Methods PC12 cells were incubated with PCB at 25 or 100 μM for different time periods. Cellular uptake and accumulation were evaluated by confocal microscopy (CFM) and flow cytometry. Intracellular spatial association of PCB with DHE- and JC-1-positive regions was assessed by colocalization analysis using Pearson’s coefficient, Manders’ coefficients, Intensity Correlation Quotient (ICQ), and Costes randomization analysis. Results PCB was progressively internalized by PC12 cells and accumulated predominantly in cytoplasmic and perinuclear regions in a time- and concentration-dependent manner. CFM and flow cytometry showed strong concordance, with significant correlations for the percentage of PCB-positive cells (r = 0.8603, p = 0.0061) and fluorescence intensity (r = 0.9563, p = 0.0002). PCB showed substantial spatial overlap with DHE-positive regions, with high Manders’ coefficients (M1 = 0.99; M2 = 0.91) and positive ICQ values.
